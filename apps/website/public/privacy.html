Privacy

heybe.dev website and waitlist · Last updated October 22, 2026

This page covers the heybe.dev website. The Heybe app itself runs on your computer; its privacy design is described in the project README.

What we collect

  • Waitlist: if you join, we store your email address, the time you signed up, and the page language. To stop abuse we also store a one-way hash of your IP address (not the address itself), used only for rate limiting.
  • No analytics, no ads, no tracking cookies. The site loads no third-party scripts or fonts.
  • Our hosting provider, Cloudflare, processes standard request data (such as IP address) to deliver and protect the site.

How we use it

Only to invite you to try Heybe when it is ready. We do not send newsletters, we do not sell or share your email, and we do not use it for advertising.

Deleting your data

To be removed from the waitlist, open an issue titled "waitlist removal" at github.com/Slovian42/Heybe without posting your email publicly, and we will reach out privately; or reply to any email we send you. We delete waitlist data once the early-access program ends.

Who we are

Heybe is made by Slovian Inc.

本页说明 heybe.dev 网站如何处理数据。Heybe 应用本身在你的电脑上运行。
